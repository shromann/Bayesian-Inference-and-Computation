# History of Bayesian Computation

> [!abstract] One-line summary
> Bayesian statistics existed as a theory for two centuries but was almost unusable until cheap computing made [[Monte Carlo Integration|Monte Carlo methods]] practical.

## Timeline
- **1763–1984:** Bayesian statistics is restricted to very simple analyses where exact integrations are possible. *Almost useless as a general statistical method.*
- **1984–2016:** Complex integrations become trivial thanks to cheap computing power and powerful Monte Carlo methods. Bayesian statistics becomes mainstream.
- **2016–now:** New computational ML/AI tools further transform Bayesian modelling; generalisations ("post-Bayesian inference") develop. The field is developing rapidly.

## Why it matters
Bayesian development is closely linked to computational capability. The reason Bayesian methods were not viable before the early 1990s is exactly the [[Normalising Constant]] / integration problem — see [[Posterior Inference]] slide "lots of difficult algebra even for simple models".
